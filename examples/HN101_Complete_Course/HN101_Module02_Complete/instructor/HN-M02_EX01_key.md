## Study-note answers

### Lesson 2.1

- L21-P01: 90 × .005 = 0.45 kg/week initially. Arithmetic does not establish suitability.
- L21-P02: Fixed 10 weeks; proportional continuous crossing approximately 10.23, first qualifying whole week 11.
- L21-P03: Targets 0.7 and 1.4%; achieved approximately 0.7 and 1.0%/week in N02.
- L21-P04: (2,240 − 400)/64 = 28.75 kcal/kg FFM/day. TDEE contains more than exercise expenditure and would make it a different calculation. No diagnosis from this number alone.
- L21-P05: Zero planned deficit days, seven calendar days. This does not establish exact energy balance during a real break.
- CUT-A/B/C: 2,240/2,380/2,080 kcal/day; planned differences 560/420/520.

### Lesson 2.2

- L22-P01: BULK-B adds 280 kcal/day; seven-day planned difference 1,960 kcal.
- L22-P02: 0.2 kg/week initially; total-mass target, not identified muscle gain.
- L22-P03: 21 randomized, 17 completed; preserve allocation versus completion.
- L22-P04: Carbohydrate 430 g/day with P=160, fat=80, E=3,080.
- L22-P05: 280/2,800 = 10%, while 280/3,080 ≈ 9.09%. Reference values differ.
- BULK-LAB: A adds 980 kcal/week; C adds 2,940. Exact muscle gains are unknown. Statement 1 is model arithmetic; 2 is source-based study information; 3 is unsupported; 4 is a limitation of scale-only inference.

### Lesson 2.3

- L23-P01: 20% → 17.5%, a 2.5-percentage-point decrease; 12.5% relative decrease.
- L23-P02: End BF = 100×14/79 ≈ 17.72%; total −1 kg, fat −2 kg, FFM +1 kg.
- L23-P03: No. Different components have different energy densities; a mass balance is not a stored-energy balance.
- L23-P04: Supported: improved lean/fat outcomes can coexist under the studied deficit/training/protein conditions. Unsupported: everyone should reproduce the aggressive protocol or will gain exactly the same muscle.
- L23-P05: No; goals, training history, measurement reliability, health, and preferences are additional considerations.
- Lab reports: unchanged total does not preclude opposite compartment changes; FFM is not identical to muscle protein; strength change alone does not identify energy balance.

### Lesson 2.4

- L24-P01: BW rule 128–176 g/day; specialized FFM rule 147.2–198.4.
- L24-P02: No exact FFM-based output without FFM or an explicitly authorized input estimate; do not borrow BW as if equivalent.
- L24-P03: Approximately 53.33 g/meal. No universal 30 g absorption cap is established.
- L24-P04: A confidence interval concerns uncertainty in the estimated population model parameter; it is not a guarantee or individual requirement interval.
- L24-P05: 30 g serving contains 24 g protein; masses are not interchangeable.

### Lesson 2.5

- L25-P01: C=242.5 g/day. F in this macro equation is dietary-fat grams, not FFM.
- L25-P02: Algebraic C=−50 g, infeasible. Clamping to zero leaves E=1,700, exceeding 1,500 by 200.
- L25-P03: 4.5 P, 42 C, 0.75 fat grams; 192.75 kcal under 4/4/9. No cooking yield provided.
- L25-P04: 24 g protein in 30 g powder.
- L25-P05: 24 g/day during the specified loading example versus assigned 5 g/day maintenance; not an instruction to load or to use 24 permanently.
- L25-P06: CAFF-A 380 mg; CAFF-B 440 mg; 3 mg/kg at 80 kg is 240 mg; 6 mg/kg is 480 mg. All are arithmetic scenarios, not personal authorization.

## Suggested marking approach

Award setup and interpretation credit separately. Correct multiplication with the wrong case, denominator, unit, or food state is not a correct answer. Give full credit for identifying genuine insufficient information when a numeric output is not supported. Do not reward unsupported clinical diagnoses or dietary prescriptions.

Suggested course mastery threshold: 80/100, with review of any unresolved BW/FFM, cooked/dry, missing/zero, or mg/g confusion. This threshold is a pedagogical choice.
