Nutrition Lab Bench

Keep the case, denominator, food state, and units attached to the calculation. Outputs below are conditional arithmetic for fictional cases, not individualized prescriptions.

No external scripts, accounts, or network requests. Supplement dosing is discussed in the lessons; this worksheet does not generate a supplement prescription.

01 · Planned energy

Negative means deficit, positive means surplus. The percentage denominator is estimated TDEE. This tool does not predict tissue gain or loss.

02 · Protein denominators

1.6–2.2 g/kg BW is the general planning exercise. 2.3–3.1 g/kg FFM is a separate specialized cutting context. The ranges are not confidence intervals or universal requirements.

03 · Macro feasibility

C = (E − 4P − 9F)/4. Infeasible constraints are rejected rather than converted into a negative-carbohydrate eating instruction. Arithmetic closure does not establish diet quality.

04 · Portion and food state

Synthetic records only. Selecting a state does not convert cooked mass into dry mass. No cooking yield is supplied. A powder portion is not entirely protein.
