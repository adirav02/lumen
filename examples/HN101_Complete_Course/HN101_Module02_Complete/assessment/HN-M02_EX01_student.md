# Module 2 Examination — Nutrition and Body-Composition Reasoning

Time: 75 minutes. Total: 100 points. Calculator permitted. Show units and assumptions. All numerical cases are synthetic. The exam assesses conditional calculations and interpretation, not personal medical or nutrition prescribing.

## Formula sheet

Planned intake = estimated TDEE × (1 + signed percentage/100).

Fixed-rate duration = mass difference / absolute rate. Proportional model: W_n = W_0(1 − r)^n.

FFM estimate = BW × (1 − body-fat fraction). Body-fat percentage = 100 × FM / BW.

Macro energy = 4P + 4C + 9F; all macros in grams. C = (E − 4P − 9F)/4.

Per-portion nutrient = nutrient per 100 g × portion mass/100.

Illustrative EA = (EI − specified exercise expenditure)/FFM.

Protein rules supplied in this module: 1.6–2.2 g/kg BW/day for the general planning exercise; 2.3–3.1 g/kg FFM/day for the separate specialized cutting exercise. These are context-dependent rules, not interchangeable universal prescriptions.

## Short questions — 5 points each

**M02-Q01.** CUT-B uses estimated expenditure 2,800 kcal/day and a planned 15% deficit. Calculate planned intake and daily planned difference.

**M02-Q02.** For an 80 kg case, translate an assigned 0.5% weekly loss target into an initial absolute rate. Does it guarantee that amount of fat loss?

**M02-Q03.** In the Garthe trial discussed in Lesson 2.1, distinguish the targeted faster-group rate from its reported achieved rate.

**M02-Q04.** BULK-B uses estimated expenditure 2,800 and an assigned 10% surplus. Calculate planned intake. Can you infer exact weekly muscle gain?

**M02-Q05.** In N05, how many participants were randomized and how many completed? Explain why the two counts should not be swapped.

**M02-Q06.** RECOMP-A goes from 16 kg fat + 64 kg FFM to 14 kg fat + 66 kg FFM. Calculate total mass and body-fat percentage at each time.

**M02-Q07.** For PROTEIN-A, BW = 80 kg and FFM = 64 kg. Calculate the specialized 2.3–3.1 g/kg FFM/day range and name its denominator.

**M02-Q08.** Does a population-level estimated protein breakpoint establish a universal 30 g meal absorption cap? Explain the mismatch between those concepts.

**M02-Q09.** MACRO-CUT uses 2,240 kcal/day, 160 g protein, and 70 g fat. Calculate carbohydrate grams under the simplified factors.

**M02-Q10.** A 30 g portion of POWDER-A is 80% protein by mass. Calculate protein mass. State whether a 30 g serving and 30 g protein are identical.

## Extended questions — 10 points each

**M02-Q11 — Conflicting constraints.** A proposed plan specifies 1,500 kcal, 200 g protein, and 100 g dietary fat. Calculate the remaining carbohydrate allowance algebraically, interpret the result, and explain why replacing it with zero does not fix the plan.

**M02-Q12 — Food state and evidence.** GRAIN-COOKED has 3 g protein, 28 g carbohydrate, and 0.5 g fat per 100 g cooked. Calculate macros and simplified energy for 150 g cooked. Can these values identify the dry mass before cooking? Explain.

**M02-Q13 — Compare two duration models.** Starting from 80 kg and aiming for 76 kg, compare (a) fixed 0.4 kg/week loss and (b) a model multiplying current mass by 0.995 each week. Calculate the crossing time for each and identify the first whole week at or below 76 in model (b). Explain why neither is a guaranteed biological forecast.

**M02-Q14 — Units, protocol, and totals.** An 80 kg arithmetic case considers creatine loading at 0.3 g/kg/day and a separate maintenance example of 5 g/day. Calculate loading grams and distinguish protocols. CAFF-B consists of two 90 mg coffees, 200 mg from another product, and a 60 mg beverage. Calculate total caffeine and explain why being able to calculate it does not establish individual safety.

**M02-Q15 — Integrated critique.** Audit this report. Identify five distinct errors or unsupported claims and repair each:

> “My FFM is unknown, so I used 80 kg total mass with the FFM protein rule. A 20% calorie deficit guarantees 20% weekly fat loss. My scale stayed at 80 kg, proving no recomposition. I use dry-food nutrient values for cooked portions because both are grams. My supplement does not disclose caffeine, so its caffeine contribution is zero.”
