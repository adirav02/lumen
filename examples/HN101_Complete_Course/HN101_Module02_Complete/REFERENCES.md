## Evidence boundaries

- Recommendations from contest-preparation and off-season reviews are context-dependent, not universal clinical thresholds.
- Assigned deficit/surplus percentages do not determine the exact tissue composition of weight change.
- The body-fat and FFM examples are hypothetical compartment values, not measurement validations.
- A protein breakpoint confidence interval is not a coverage interval for individual needs.
- Dose calculations for creatine and caffeine illustrate units/protocols; they do not authorize personal use.
- Research-intended intake and achieved physiological exposure can differ. Targeted and achieved rates are labeled separately.
- This packet does not replace the original papers or imply all papers were read in full.
