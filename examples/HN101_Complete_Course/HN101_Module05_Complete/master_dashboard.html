HN-101 — Master Course Dashboard

Plan, inspect, and export a scenario. Four tools combine progression, a mesocycle template, recovery time arithmetic, and nutrition/training integration. All defaults are fictional. Outputs are calculations or proposals, never proof of completed training or measured biological outcomes.

Inputs are not saved or transmitted. Export deliberately creates a local file.

1. Double progression

Two distinct consecutive exposures at the same load/setup. Every prescribed set must reach the upper target with at least the minimum RIR. Load is total external kg, not per-side kg.



Two-exposure/all-set logic is a course policy, not a universal optimum. Missing data never become zero. A bodyweight exercise requires a separate load convention.

2. Six-week planning template

Direct sets, not fractional or whole-body counts. Conditional proposal only. Week6=ceil(week5×0.5); no individualized MRV or automatic escalation.

3. Calendar and recovery worksheet

Sessions must start and end on the same day. Sleep opportunity has a separate overnight convention.


Gap does not prove recovery. Duration×sessionRPE gives arbitrary units, not calories or RIR. Sleep opportunity is not measured sleep.

4. Integrated nutrition and training scenario


The multiplier is assumed inclusive of habitual/planned activity; do not add included gross cardio calories again. Macro factors are user-selected assumptions. Static trend proxy uses7700 kcal/kg and is not true measured TDEE. Candidate exercises require setup/ROM/equipment/tolerability review and separate load baselines. The one-category ramp is not automatically added to this whole plan.
