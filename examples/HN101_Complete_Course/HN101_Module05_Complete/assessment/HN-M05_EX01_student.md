# HN-M05-EX01 — Programming, Recovery, and Integration

Time 75 minutes. Total 100 points: questions 1–10 carry 5 points each; questions 11–15 carry 10 points each. Calculator permitted. All cases are synthetic. Show units, assumptions, and case identity. Attempt before opening instructor/.

## Short questions — 50 points

1. DP-A uses total load 70 kg and total increment 2.5 kg. Both distinct consecutive exposures have three sets of 12 repetitions, with 2 RIR on every set. Range 8–12, minimum RIR 2, same setup/ROM, no concern. Give the course-policy decision, proposed load, and percentage increase.
2. Change only the current final set to 10 repetitions. Does the previous qualifying exposure alone authorize an increase? Explain.
3. Current repetitions are 12,12,12 but current RIR is missing. Should the system impute zero, assume the minimum, or withhold a proposal? Explain.
4. Give MESO-A's proposed six weekly direct-set counts and total. Name the target/counting scope.
5. Calculate MESO-A's week 6 reduction relative to week 5 after ceiling rounding. Is it exactly 50%?
6. Does the 2024 trial of one week without resistance training prove that every reduced-volume deload improves hypertrophy? Distinguish intervention and claim.
7. Resistance training is 18:00–19:00 and cardio 21:00–21:30. Give end-to-start gap and start-to-start interval. Does either prove complete recovery?
8. Resistance training is 18:00–19:00 and cardio 18:45–19:15. Calculate overlap. Compare cardio beginning exactly 19:00.
9. Calculate session-load products for 45 minutes at session RPE 7 and 30 minutes at session RPE 4, and their sum. Does session RPE 7 automatically mean 3 RIR?
10. Does 23:00–07:00 establish eight hours asleep? Explain the measurement distinction and one missing factor.

## Applied questions — 50 points

11. **CAP-A energy chain.** Use 80 kg, 180 cm, 25 years and supplied MSJ branch constant+5. Calculate predicted RMR, TDEE scenario at multiplier 1.55, and planned intake at−10%. Explain why adding gross cardio energy again may double count. Do not round between steps.
12. **Macro audit.** For CAP-A choose protein 1.8 g/kg body mass and fat 0.8 g/kg body mass. Calculate grams of protein, fat, and remaining carbohydrate using 4/9/4 kcal/g. Recalculate carbohydrate for CAP-B's explicit TDEE override 2800 with the same−10% adjustment. Name which inputs are assumptions rather than measured needs.
13. **Trend interpretation.** Adjacent complete seven-day means are 80.2 and 79.8 kg. Give mean difference and relative percentage from the first mean. Using assigned intake 2517.975 and static 7700 kcal/kg, calculate the back-calculated expenditure proxy. Explain why neither exact fat loss nor true TDEE is established.
14. **Review and revise.** A six-week template proposes adding sets, but a learner reports two short-sleep nights and lower first-set repetitions; current RIR is unknown and exercise order changed. Write an observation-based review with two alternative explanations, one reasonable provisional decision, and two additional data fields. Do not diagnose overtraining or calculate exact MRV.
15. **Dashboard audit.** A report says: “The four-day template has 48 sets per muscle; the planner proved my MRV; a shared muscle tag makes substitutes identical; exporting the plan proves completion.” Correct all four statements and explain the corresponding quantity/status distinctions.

The 100-point module exam is separate from the final capstone project.
