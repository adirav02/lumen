# HN-101 — Final Course Capstone

## Assignment

Produce a reviewable six-week nutrition/training plan for a fictional adult or a consenting self-selected adult scenario. This is an academic planning exercise, not a requirement to execute a personal diet or loaded training program. Do not infer a medical condition or attempt rehabilitation.

Use CAP-A for a fully supplied calculation case, CAP-B to test an alternative expenditure assumption, or define another case with explicit inputs. You may use the dashboard, but independently audit its calculations and labels. A polished screenshot without assumptions is not sufficient.

## Required submissions

1. **One-page proposal.** State goal, relevant constraints, expenditure/intake scenario, macro allocation, chosen training structure, aerobic placement, and review date. Distinguish supplied assumptions from measurements.
2. **Calculation appendix.** Record equation/branch, input units, optional body-fat/FFM assumption, multiplier versus supplied TDEE override, intake adjustment, macro closure, rounding, and any alternative estimate. A negative macro remainder must be corrected transparently rather than hidden.
3. **Weekly calendar.** Place resistance and aerobic sessions alongside actual or fictional commitments. Calculate at least one end-to-start gap and include a sleep-opportunity window. Identify the decision if sessions conflict.
4. **Exercise and execution sheet.** Choose candidates for the major movement tasks present in your schedule. For each, state apparatus, load convention, ROM marker, sets, repetitions, effort convention, and stopping criterion. Include two substitutions with preserved/changed/unknown features, using Module 4's framework.
5. **Progression contract and mesocycle.** Apply a stated progression policy to two distinct synthetic exposures. Provide a six-week proposal for one selected direct-set category, including rounding and a conditional hold/review rule. Do not automatically add that ramp to every exercise.
6. **Monitoring and revision log.** Use TREND-A or an explicit complete synthetic time series. Compare adjacent means, distinguish body-mass change from tissue change, and show one proposed revision with rationale. Keep planned, completed, and revised entries separate.
7. **Evidence and uncertainty note.** Cite at least five relevant source records across the course. Include one case requiring abstention or more information. Explain two claims the available data cannot establish.

## Case variants for the oral defense

- Equipment variant: the cable station is unavailable; compare two substitutes without claiming exact profile equivalence.
- Data variant: one RIR entry or daily mass is missing; state which calculations/decisions become unsupported.
- Energy variant: a TDEE multiplier already includes cardio, yet the plan adds gross cardio calories again; correct the boundary.
- Recovery variant: a session performs poorly after changed order and reduced sleep opportunity; revise the immediate plan without diagnosing from one session.
- Authority variant: an archived memo says every session must increase load. Explain why the current policy and actual observations govern.
