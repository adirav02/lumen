# HN-101 — Cumulative Final Examination

Time 120 minutes.20 questions×5 points=100. Calculator permitted. All cases are synthetic. Show calculation, units, and one material limitation where asked. This examination samples all five modules; its answer key must remain outside a blind student corpus.

1. **Energy balance:** five days each have intake 2500 and expenditure 2800 kcal/day; two days each have intake 3100 and expenditure 2700. Calculate weekly and average daily balance. Does this identify exact body-mass change?
2. **Equation identity:** use MSJ=10W+6.25H−5A+s with 80 kg, 180 cm, 25 years. Calculate both supplied branches s=+5 and s=−161. May a system silently pick one when the branch is unspecified?
3. **MET boundary:** for an assigned 6-MET activity, 80 kg and 30 minutes, calculate gross energy using MET×3.5×kg/200×minutes. Calculate the incremental above 1-MET amount using the same convention. Explain why gross energy may double count resting energy in an inclusive total.
4. **Error cancellation:** two expenditure predictions have signed errors+200 and−200 kcal/day. Give mean signed error and mean absolute error. Does the first prove perfect accuracy?
5. **Cutting:** a supplied TDEE estimate 2800 and planned 20% deficit produce what intake and planned difference? Does that prove a particular weekly fat-loss rate?
6. **Protein denominator:** for 80 kg body mass and 64 kg FFM, calculate 1.8 g/kg body mass and 2.5 g/kg FFM. Why must the denominators remain explicit?
7. **Macro closure:** a2400 kcal plan assigns 160 g protein and 80 g fat. Calculate remaining carbohydrate using 4/9/4 kcal/g. Is negative carbohydrate a valid way to repair an overallocated plan?
8. **Composition:** body mass stays 80 kg while supplied fat mass changes 16→14 kg and FFM 64→66 kg. Calculate final body-fat percentage. Does FFM gain prove the same gain in muscle protein?
9. **Estimated 1RM:** using Epley-style L(1+r/30), calculate 80 kg×8 entered repetitions. Is the result a measured maximum or an input-independent fact?
10. **Scale identity:** under the course set convention RPE≈10−RIR, convert 2 RIR. May that result be applied automatically to an overall session RPE scale?
11. **Volume convention:** direct setsD=2 and indirect setsI=6. Calculate D+alphaI for alpha 0,0.5,1. Is the sum of all muscles' counts necessarily the physical-set total?
12. **Rest boundary:** four 40-second work sets with 120-second rests only between adjacent sets take how long? Does the block include an extra rest after the last set?
13. **Moment geometry:** force 100 N acts at radius 0.50 m, with perpendicular pivot-to-force-line distance 0.30 m. Calculate moment magnitude. Is the result automatically individual muscle force?
14. **Two-joint mechanics:** with other positions comparable, contrast hip-flexion effects on biarticular hamstring length and rectus-femoris length. Name the hamstring exception that does not cross the hip.
15. **Task-specific cues:** explain why a selected bench scapular setup is not a universal requirement to pin the scapula down throughout overhead elevation. Does elbow flare 60° guarantee safety?
16. **Substitution:** compare an overhead triceps extension with a pressdown. State one shared primary action, one changed position, and why equal muscle tags do not establish exact mechanical equivalence.
17. **Progression:** DP-A meets the stated upper-repetition and minimum-RIR conditions across two distinct comparable complete exposures at 70 kg, with total increment 2.5 kg and no concern. Give the proposal. Then change only the current final set to 10 repetitions under an 8–12 range; give the revised decision.
18. **Mesocycle rounding:** given week 5 count 11, week 6=ceil(0.5×week 5), calculate retained sets and percentage reduction. Does the template identify exact personal MRV?
19. **Concurrent training and sleep:** resistance 18:00–19:00, cardio 21:00–21:30, bed 23:00 and wake 07:00. Calculate gap and sleep opportunity. State why neither guarantees complete physiological recovery.
20. **Integrated audit:** CAP-A has predicted RMR 1805, inclusive multiplier 1.55, intake adjustment−10%, body mass 80 kg, protein factor 1.8 and fat factor 0.8 g/kg. Calculate intake and remaining carbohydrate with 4/9/4. Does export of that proposal establish adherence or actual energy balance?

Suggested mastery threshold 75/100 is an authored course convention. Final-exam results are distinct from module exams and the final project; no external accreditation is claimed.
