# HN 101 Fitness Nutrition and Strength

An English course source collection about energy balance, sports nutrition, strength, hypertrophy, biomechanics, training design, and recovery. It includes teaching material, worked examples, exercise tools, question papers, and worked solutions in several file formats.

These are authored educational examples. Numerical cases are fictional and do not represent actual learner performance or medical measurements. The supplied folders reflect ordinary course authoring. They have not been processed into a LUMEN catalog or learner workspace.
