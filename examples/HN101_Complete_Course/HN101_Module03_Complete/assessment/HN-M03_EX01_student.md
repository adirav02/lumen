# Module 3 Examination — Training Stimulus and Dose

Time: 75 minutes. Total: 100 points. Calculator permitted. Show definitions, units, and assumptions. All numerical cases are synthetic unless a named research source is explicitly requested. No physical testing is required.

## Formula sheet

- Epley-style estimate: L(1 + r/30).
- Brzycki estimate: 36L/(37 − r).
- Percentage load: estimated 1RM × fraction.
- RIR-based RPE convention: approximately 10 − RIR for the course range 0–4 RIR.
- Signed reserve error = estimated reserve − supplied additional valid repetitions.
- Fractional set count: D + αI, with α specified by the question.
- Volume-load: sum of load × completed valid repetitions under a consistent load convention.
- Set-block time: ns + (n − 1)t, with no final rest included.

## Short questions — 5 points each

**M03-Q01.** Does severe soreness after a novel workout establish more long-term hypertrophy than a session with little soreness? State what is and is not observed.

**M03-Q02.** Distinguish motor-unit recruitment from rate coding. Does the conceptual diagram give a measured numerical recruitment threshold?

**M03-Q03.** A person lifts 80 kg when their supplied task-specific 1RM is 100 kg. Calculate relative load. Does this identify effort or RIR without a stopping-point record?

**M03-Q04.** Calculate the Epley-style estimate for MAX-A: 80 kg for 8 repetitions. State whether the output is measured capacity.

**M03-Q05.** Calculate the Brzycki estimate for MAX-A. Does selecting a different formula prove physiological change?

**M03-Q06.** Convert 2 and 3 RIR into the specified RIR-based RPE convention. Would a Borg 6–20 score of 8 automatically have the same meaning?

**M03-Q07.** RIR-A estimates 2 repetitions in reserve; a supplied hypothetical continuation record contains 4 valid repetitions. Calculate signed error and interpret its direction.

**M03-Q08.** For a target with D=2 and I=6, calculate direct-only, fractional α=.5, and total α=1 counts.

**M03-Q09.** WEEK-A has four sessions. Biceps receives direct curls in U1, indirect rows in U1, and indirect pulldowns in U2. Give program, direct-biceps, and contributing-biceps exposure frequencies.

**M03-Q10.** Under eccentric–bottom–concentric–top notation, calculate the assigned duration of 10 repetitions at 3–1–1–0. Why is 2–0–X–0 insufficient for exact timing?

## Extended questions — 10 points each

**M03-Q11 — Prediction, reserve, and rounding.** Calculate 75% of MAX-A's Epley-style estimated 1RM and round to the nearest 2.5 kg total increment. Then compare MAX-C, which explicitly hypothesizes 8 completed repetitions + 2 estimated RIR = 10 possible repetitions. Calculate its equation estimate and explain why this is not proof of a new measured maximum.

**M03-Q12 — Versioned logs.** WEEK-A has 28 physical sets and biceps D=2, I=6. WEEK-B only increases curls from 2 to 4 sets. Calculate WEEK-B physical sets, biceps fractional count at α=.5, and triceps fractional count if its original D=2, I=6 are unchanged. Can these logs determine personal MRV? Explain.

**M03-Q13 — Performance and time.** REST-A uses 80 kg for 10, 8, and 6 repetitions. REST-B uses 80 kg for 10, 10, and 9. Calculate volume-load for both. Separately time a block of four 40-second sets with 120 seconds only between sets. Distinguish the block boundary from full-session duration.

**M03-Q14 — Reconciling evidence.** Explain why these statements can be compatible: a failure/non-failure synthesis does not require failure on every set; an exploratory RIR meta-regression associates closer estimated RIR with hypertrophy; an overview says exact optimal RIR targets cannot be established precisely. Identify one limitation of the exploratory evidence and distinguish hypertrophy from strength outcomes.

**M03-Q15 — Integrated audit.** Identify and repair five distinct errors in this report:

> “My missing RIR field means zero reserve. My fractional biceps count of five can be compared directly with any direct-only MRV table. A heavier 1RM proves exactly two kilograms of new muscle. TEMPO-B's X means zero seconds. The three-set data prove that resting longer always causes more growth for everyone.”
