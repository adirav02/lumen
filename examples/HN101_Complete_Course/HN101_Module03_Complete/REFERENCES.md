## Source and model boundaries

- The five main figures are conceptual or model-generated; none is a reproduced research plot or participant dataset.
- H=min(r,max(0,5−RIR)) is an explicit teaching representation of a heuristic, not a validated growth measurement.
- The course tool's 2–10 repetition domain and nearest-increment rounding rule are software design choices.
- D+.5I is a counting convention. A weight of .5 is not an observed half-share of hypertrophy for every indirect set.
- MV/MEV/MAV/MRV terminology is described as the provider's coaching framework. A static log does not identify personal thresholds.
- Online and journal-volume dates can differ. T12 is online 2025 and volume 2026; this is not evidence of two independent studies.
- Limited access is disclosed rather than presented as a complete critical appraisal of every source.
