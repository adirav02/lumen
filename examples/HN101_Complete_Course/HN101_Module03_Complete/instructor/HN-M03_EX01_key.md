## Practice solutions

### Lesson 3.1

- L31-P01: Recruitment changes active motor units; rate coding changes discharge behavior of already active units.
- L31-P02: No. T01 is a conceptual research synthesis, not proof of three equally necessary independent ingredients.
- L31-P03: External mass does not supply internal fiber tension; joint geometry, movement, muscle forces, and load sharing matter.
- L31-P04: Acute synthesis can reflect repair/remodeling; a large initial signal is not an exact long-term hypertrophy measurement.
- L31-P05: No. Technique, neural factors, and other contributors are not isolated.
- MECH-LAB: Need comparable longitudinal structural outcomes and measurement conditions; do not fabricate a soreness-growth conversion. M03-F01 is a conceptual diagram.

### Lesson 3.2
