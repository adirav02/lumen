Training Dose Lab

A number is useful when its task, units, and assumptions travel with it. These tools illustrate the course equations using fictional defaults.

No personal load prescription, maximal test, or failure test is required. Predictions, scale conventions, counting rules, and time arithmetic are different kinds of output. No data leave this file.

01 · Estimated 1RM

Epley-style L(1+r/30); Brzycki 36L/(37−r). Domain 2–10 is a course design limit. RIR is not automatically added. Nearest total increment is used; ties round upward. Outputs do not establish measured capacity or readiness to lift them.

02 · RIR-based RPE

RPE≈10−RIR within this course's limited range. This is not a converter for Borg 6–20, pain ratings, or session RPE. An unknown reserve must remain unknown.

03 · Per-muscle set accounting

V=D+αI. This does not calculate personal MRV, measured growth, or the unique physical-set total across all muscles. Do not compare with a direct-only threshold unless definitions are aligned.

04 · Set-block time

Time=ns+(n−1)t. No final rest, warm-up, transitions, or setup are included. X in tempo notation is not a numeric duration.
