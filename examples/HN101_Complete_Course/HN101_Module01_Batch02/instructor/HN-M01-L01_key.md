## Concept check and practice solutions

**C01:** 70 × 24 = 1,680 kcal. The assumption is that the rate remains constant for the full 24 hours.

**LAB01:** A, B, C, D, respectively. Scenario A still depends on valid measurement procedures. A “BMR” label alone does not demonstrate basal conditions.

**Change-one-variable exercise:** The meal introduces food-related expenditure and violates basal conditions; shivering indicates a non-thermoneutral environment; a prediction-equation output is a prediction rather than a respiratory measurement on that individual. The scenarios supply no numerical correction factor.

**P01:** Five days contribute −1,500 kcal; two days contribute +800. Weekly balance is −700 kcal, and average daily balance is −100 kcal/day.

**P02:** Total expenditure for the same day is missing. Subtracting activity expenditure alone from intake does not establish energy balance. The device's definition and accuracy also require attention.

**P03:** Acceptable issues include: whether both outputs refer to the same quantity; population suitability of the equation; laboratory protocol and equipment quality; accuracy of calculator inputs; and whether timing or physiological conditions differ. A discrepancy alone does not identify its cause.
