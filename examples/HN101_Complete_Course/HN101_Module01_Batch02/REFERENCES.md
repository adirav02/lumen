## Attribution rules

1. A model-generated value is not a measured research result.
2. A source's formula label is not a guarantee that all other publications use the same label. Retain coefficients and units.
3. The R02 historical sample excluded trained athletes. Later validation in athletes is a different research question.
4. FFM-370-21.6 naming varies across papers, including Cunningham 1991 and Katch–McArdle labels. This packet identifies the equation directly rather than resolving every historical naming convention.
5. The assigned TEF coefficients, body-fat sensitivity range, activity-factor example, and k sensitivity values are classroom assumptions. They are not evidence-based personal prescriptions.
6. The blueprint's phrase “true TDEE” has been replaced by “conditional TDEE estimate.” No food log plus scale series directly identifies a unique physiological truth without assumptions.

For the original Lesson 1.1 references, retain its own S1–S5 identifiers; they are local to that lesson. Do not treat S1 and R01 as the same citation ID.
