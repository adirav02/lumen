# Exercise Library — 31 Authored Observation Cards

These cards are course teaching aids. They are not measured resistance profiles, clinical prescriptions, or universal technique rules. Equipment tags are a search convention. Treat combined tags such as dumbbells_bench as requiring both items. Establish a separate load baseline after a substitution. All cards require individual tolerability to be assessed; the filter does not establish it.


## SQ01 — High-bar squat

**Action:** knee_hip_extension. **Equipment tag:** barbell.

**Secondary position:** Bar high on upper back.

**Setup:** Set rack, stance, and whole-foot support.

**Counted range:** Use a repeatable depth marker appropriate to the task.

**Observe:** Foot support, knee path, torso behavior, and end-set depth.

**Profile limitation:** Vertical bar weight; joint moments require geometry and other forces.


## SQ02 — Low-bar squat

**Action:** knee_hip_extension. **Equipment tag:** barbell.

**Secondary position:** Lower posterior shoulder bar position.

**Setup:** Establish a comfortable grip and stable bar support.

**Counted range:** Log the chosen depth and stance.

**Observe:** Balance and reproducible torso inclination.

**Profile limitation:** Different bar position can change linked-segment geometry; no isolation claim.


## SQ03 — Hack squat

**Action:** knee_hip_extension. **Equipment tag:** machine.

**Secondary position:** Back/shoulder support depends on apparatus.

**Setup:** Set stops and platform position before loading.

**Counted range:** Record sled travel and joint-position landmarks.

**Observe:** Support contact, foot pressure, controlled reversals.

**Profile limitation:** Machine path and mass conventions needed; stack label is insufficient.


## SQ04 — Leg press

**Action:** knee_hip_extension. **Equipment tag:** machine.

**Secondary position:** Seated or reclined machine-specific support.

**Setup:** Record seat setting, platform, and stops.

**Counted range:** End before losing the chosen pelvic/support relationship.

**Observe:** Pelvic motion and repeatability; avoid diagnosing from one frame.

**Profile limitation:** Rail angle, friction, moving mass, and mechanism change force.


## SQ05 — Knee extension

**Action:** knee_extension. **Equipment tag:** machine.

**Secondary position:** Hip-flexion angle must be defined.

**Setup:** Adjust axis region and pad to apparatus instructions.

**Counted range:** State knee start/end positions and reversal control.

**Observe:** Hip position and pad contact throughout the set.

**Profile limitation:** Cam and pad leverage can vary; rectus femoris also crosses hip.


## HG01 — Conventional deadlift

**Action:** hip_knee_extension. **Equipment tag:** barbell.

**Secondary position:** Narrower stance with arms outside legs.

**Setup:** Arrange load over the intended base and take a repeatable grip.

**Counted range:** Floor start to controlled standing endpoint.

**Observe:** Bar path, coordinated hip/knee extension, task consistency.

**Profile limitation:** Bar weight is only one external force component.


## HG02 — Sumo deadlift

**Action:** hip_knee_extension. **Equipment tag:** barbell.

**Secondary position:** Wider stance with arms inside legs.

**Setup:** Choose a reproducible stance and grip.

**Counted range:** Record floor start and completion rule.

**Observe:** Foot support and coordinated extension.

**Profile limitation:** Different segment geometry from conventional; not universally safer.


## HG03 — Romanian deadlift

**Action:** hip_extension. **Equipment tag:** barbell.

**Secondary position:** Chosen modest knee bend.

**Setup:** Start standing; move hips back with load controlled near legs.

**Counted range:** Stop at reproducible hip-motion endpoint, not compulsory floor contact.

**Observe:** Unplanned knee changes or new torso motion near bottom.

**Profile limitation:** External hip moment depends partly on horizontal bar-to-hip distance.


## HG04 — Barbell hip thrust

**Action:** hip_extension. **Equipment tag:** barbell_bench.

**Secondary position:** Upper back supported; knees bent.

**Setup:** Secure bench/support and load placement.

**Counted range:** Define bottom and top hip positions without chasing trunk extension.

**Observe:** Support stability and actual hip motion.

**Profile limitation:** Support and load geometry differ from RDL; not a knee-flexion task.


## HG05 — Seated leg curl

**Action:** knee_flexion. **Equipment tag:** machine.

**Secondary position:** Hip flexed relative to prone setup.

**Setup:** Record seat, thigh restraint, and pad adjustments.

**Counted range:** State knee arc and keep chosen pelvic position repeatable.

**Observe:** Pelvis movement and reduced late-set arc.

**Profile limitation:** Machine-specific profile; hip position affects biarticular hamstring length.


## HG06 — Prone leg curl

**Action:** knee_flexion. **Equipment tag:** machine.

**Secondary position:** Less hip flexion than seated setup.

**Setup:** Adjust pad and support to machine instructions.

**Counted range:** Record knee start/end landmarks.

**Observe:** Pelvic lifting or unintended hip-motion changes.

**Profile limitation:** Knee-flexion task preserved; hip position differs from seated curl.


## PR01 — Barbell bench press

**Action:** press. **Equipment tag:** barbell_bench.

**Secondary position:** Torso supported; selected scapular setup.

**Setup:** Set rack/safeties or assistance, grip, and contact points.

**Counted range:** Record touch/depth and completion landmarks.

**Observe:** Bar control, forearm arrangement, repeatable bottom.

**Profile limitation:** Vertical bar force; shoulder/elbow moments require geometry.


## PR02 — Dumbbell bench press

**Action:** press. **Equipment tag:** dumbbells_bench.

**Secondary position:** Supported torso; independent implements.

**Setup:** Define per-dumbbell mass and controlled entry/exit.

**Counted range:** State depth and hand-path landmarks.

**Observe:** Independent implement control and chosen shoulder motion.

**Profile limitation:** Gravity force direction; not identical to cable or barbell profile.


## PR03 — Incline dumbbell press

**Action:** press. **Equipment tag:** dumbbells_bench.

**Secondary position:** Torso inclined at recorded angle.

**Setup:** Record bench angle convention and per-implement load.

**Counted range:** Use repeatable bottom and top positions.

**Observe:** Arm path and torso/support contact.

**Profile limitation:** Incline changes relationship to gravity; no exact regional-growth promise.


## PR04 — Dip

**Action:** press. **Equipment tag:** dip_bars.

**Secondary position:** Supported by hands with body moving.

**Setup:** Use suitable bars or assistance and a controllable starting position.

**Counted range:** Choose tolerable shoulder/elbow range with clear endpoint.

**Observe:** Control, support, and changes in depth or swing.

**Profile limitation:** Body mass/assistance conventions differ from stack loading.


## PR05 — Cable fly

**Action:** horizontal_adduction. **Equipment tag:** cable.

**Secondary position:** Elbow bend and shoulder range recorded.

**Setup:** Record pulley height, stance, and handle path.

**Counted range:** Define shoulder start/end positions rather than hand contact alone.

**Observe:** Elbow bend drift and torso assistance.

**Profile limitation:** Cable points toward pulley; name does not establish stretch loading.


## PR06 — Dumbbell overhead press

**Action:** press. **Equipment tag:** dumbbells.

**Secondary position:** Arm elevation with scapular upward rotation.

**Setup:** Select seated or standing setup and log it.

**Counted range:** Record chosen shoulder/elbow range.

**Observe:** Torso behavior, implement path, and controlled arm elevation.

**Profile limitation:** Gravity-driven implement forces; bench scapular cue does not transfer automatically.


## PR07 — Cable lateral raise

**Action:** shoulder_abduction. **Equipment tag:** cable.

**Secondary position:** Starting arm and pulley location defined.

**Setup:** Log anchor height and body position relative to pulley.

**Counted range:** Set start/end arm landmarks.

**Observe:** Torso lean and changes in force-line geometry.

**Profile limitation:** Constant tension is not constant shoulder moment.


## PU01 — Lat pulldown

**Action:** vertical_pull. **Equipment tag:** cable.

**Secondary position:** Seat and thigh restraint recorded.

**Setup:** Specify handle, grip reference, and torso setup.

**Counted range:** Record overhead and bottom landmarks.

**Observe:** Elbow path, scapular motion, and changing torso swing.

**Profile limitation:** Actual pulley/handle force line needed; grip does not isolate a lat region.


## PU02 — Chest-supported close-elbow row

**Action:** horizontal_pull. **Equipment tag:** dumbbells_bench.

**Secondary position:** Chest support; elbows nearer torso.

**Setup:** Record bench angle and handle/implement arrangement.

**Counted range:** Define reach and end position.

**Observe:** Shoulder-extension component, support contact, elbow path.

**Profile limitation:** Support reduces torso-holding demand; geometry still determines moments.


## PU03 — Chest-supported outward-elbow row

**Action:** horizontal_pull. **Equipment tag:** dumbbells_bench.

**Secondary position:** Chest support; elbows farther outward.

**Setup:** Record bench and grip setup.

**Counted range:** Define arm/scapular landmarks without forcing a fixed maximum.

**Observe:** Horizontal-abduction component and actual elbow path.

**Profile limitation:** Intended upper-back/rear-deltoid emphasis is not muscle isolation.


## PU04 — Pull-up

**Action:** vertical_pull. **Equipment tag:** pullup_bar.

**Secondary position:** Body moves relative to fixed hand support.

**Setup:** Log grip, body mass, assistance, and added load conventions.

**Counted range:** Specify bottom/top landmarks.

**Observe:** Body swing and reproducible shoulder/elbow movement.

**Profile limitation:** Body mass cannot be equated directly to pulldown stack mass.


## PU05 — Reverse fly

**Action:** horizontal_abduction. **Equipment tag:** dumbbells_bench.

**Secondary position:** Chest support optional but must be specified.

**Setup:** Choose a controllable elbow bend and support arrangement.

**Counted range:** Define arm arc and endpoint.

**Observe:** Actual shoulder motion versus torso motion.

**Profile limitation:** Different primary task from a vertical pull; gravity and leverage matter.


## IS01 — Overhead cable triceps extension

**Action:** elbow_extension. **Equipment tag:** cable.

**Secondary position:** Shoulder overhead.

**Setup:** Record pulley, arm position, grip, and support.

**Counted range:** State elbow arc with shoulder position repeatable.

**Observe:** Upper-arm drift and force direction.

**Profile limitation:** Long-head position differs from pressdown; cable profile is setup-dependent.


## IS02 — Overhead dumbbell triceps extension

**Action:** elbow_extension. **Equipment tag:** dumbbells.

**Secondary position:** Shoulder overhead.

**Setup:** Specify one/two hands and per-implement load.

**Counted range:** Choose repeatable elbow endpoints.

**Observe:** Control and ability to maintain intended arm position.

**Profile limitation:** Preserves broad overhead position but not exact cable resistance profile.


## IS03 — Cable pressdown

**Action:** elbow_extension. **Equipment tag:** cable.

**Secondary position:** Upper arm nearer side.

**Setup:** Record pulley/handle and torso setup.

**Counted range:** Specify elbow start/end positions.

**Observe:** Upper-arm and trunk drift.

**Profile limitation:** Shares elbow extension with overhead version but changes shoulder position.


## IS04 — Incline dumbbell curl

**Action:** elbow_flexion. **Equipment tag:** dumbbells_bench.

**Secondary position:** Upper arm behind torso as set up.

**Setup:** Record bench angle, arm position, and per-dumbbell mass.

**Counted range:** Define elbow range without losing intended shoulder arrangement.

**Observe:** Shoulder drift and controlled reversal.

**Profile limitation:** Gravity differs from cable force; behind-body label does not prove same profile.


## IS05 — Behind-body cable curl

**Action:** elbow_flexion. **Equipment tag:** cable.

**Secondary position:** Upper arm behind torso as set up.

**Setup:** Record pulley, stance, and shoulder position.

**Counted range:** Define elbow arc and upper-arm behavior.

**Observe:** Cable line at start/middle/end.

**Profile limitation:** Same arm position can have different external moments with a new pulley line.


## IS06 — Standing calf raise

**Action:** plantarflexion. **Equipment tag:** calf_machine.

**Secondary position:** Knee relatively extended.

**Setup:** Set support, foot position, and load convention.

**Counted range:** Record dorsiflexion and plantarflexion endpoints.

**Observe:** Knee position and controlled reversal without undefined bounce.

**Profile limitation:** Longer gastrocnemius across knee than seated posture; soleus differs anatomically.


## IS07 — Seated calf raise

**Action:** plantarflexion. **Equipment tag:** calf_machine.

**Secondary position:** Knee substantially flexed.

**Setup:** Record knee/pad setup and foot position.

**Counted range:** Record ankle arc.

**Observe:** Knee/pad movement and actual ankle travel.

**Profile limitation:** Changes gastrocnemius operating length; does not switch it completely off.


## IS08 — Cable crunch

**Action:** trunk_flexion. **Equipment tag:** cable.

**Secondary position:** Pelvis/torso behavior defined.

**Setup:** Select anchor, grip, and stance/support.

**Counted range:** Specify intended trunk-flexion arc.

**Observe:** Distinguish trunk flexion from predominantly hip hinging.

**Profile limitation:** Cable kilograms alone do not identify abdominal or spinal tissue force.
