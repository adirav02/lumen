# Module 4 — Source register and claim boundaries

Checked 2026-10-22. Text, cases, exercise cards, numerical inputs, and figures are authored teaching material. Synthetic numbers are not participant data or research-derived individual predictions. No research figure has been reproduced. URLs link to original records, textbooks, or the original provider's coaching analysis; coaching content has a different evidentiary role from a training intervention.

## R01 — Mechanics textbook

OpenStax. University Physics Volume 1, section 10.6, Torque. [Official chapter](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/10-6-torque).

Role: definition of moment, perpendicular lever arm, and force/radius angle. Applied examples in this module are independently constructed planar single-force problems. The textbook relation does not supply anatomical moment arms or individual muscle forces. Official chapter accessible during verification.

## R02 — Narrative technique review

Androulakis Korakakis P, Wolf M, Coleman M, et al. Optimizing Resistance Training Technique to Maximize Muscle Hypertrophy: A Narrative Review. Journal of Functional Morphology and Kinesiology,9(1),9. DOI10.3390/jfmk9010009. [PMC record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10801605/). [Publisher](https://www.mdpi.com/2411-5142/9/1/9).

Role: background reading on technique and hypertrophy, not a validated personalized exercise-ranking algorithm. Citation has a late-2023 online/2024 issue-year convention; these are not two separate studies. Access limitation: PMC returned a browser check and publisher returned429 during this module's verification. No new granular result was inferred from inaccessible full text.

## R03 — Original training intervention

Wolf M, Androulakis Korakakis P, Piñero A, et al.(2025). Lengthened partial repetitions elicit similar muscular adaptations as full range of motion repetitions during resistance training in trained individuals. PeerJ13:e18904. DOI10.7717/peerj.18904. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11829627/).

Role: measured upper-body adaptations under the studied lengthened-partial versus full-ROM protocol. It is not a trial of arbitrary partials in every exercise or a universal superiority claim. Full text accessible. Preserve intervention, outcome, and population when summarizing.

## R04 — Original coaching analysis

Nuckols G. Squat Mechanics — The Red Pill. Stronger by Science. [Article](https://www.strongerbyscience.com/squat-mechanics-the-red-pill/).

Role: squat mechanics reasoning and coaching context. It does not provide universal anatomical thresholds or an injury-prediction rule. Original article available through search retrieval. The authored squat cases are not copied from it.

## R05 — Original coaching guide

Nuckols G. How to Deadlift: The Definitive Guide. Stronger by Science. [Guide](https://www.strongerbyscience.com/how-to-deadlift/).

Role: practical deadlift setup and technique context. It is not a clinical diagnosis tool, or proof that one stance minimizes every tissue load. Guide accessible; module's force cases are independently assigned.

## R06 — Original seated/prone knee-flexion intervention

Maeo S, Huang M, Wu Y, et al.(2021). Greater Hamstrings Muscle Hypertrophy but Similar Damage Protection after Training at Long versus Short Muscle Lengths. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise 53(4):825–837. DOI10.1249/MSS.0000000000002523. [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33009197/). [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7969179/).

Role: seated versus prone knee-flexion comparison and distinction between biarticular and monoarticular hamstrings. PubMed abstract accessible; direct PMC page returned browser verification. Do not generalize the comparison to all machines or all posterior-chain exercises.

## R07 — Original bench coaching guide

Nuckols G. How to Bench Press: The Definitive Guide. Stronger by Science. [Guide](https://www.strongerbyscience.com/how-to-bench/).

Role: bench setup and bar-path coaching. Bench-specific cues are not universal overhead rules. No elbow-angle interval in this module is claimed to guarantee injury prevention. Guide accessible.

## R08 — Original overhead/neutral-arm elbow-extension intervention

Maeo S, Wu Y, Huang M, et al.(2023 issue;2022 online). Triceps brachii hypertrophy is substantially greater after elbow extension training performed in the overhead versus neutral arm position. European Journal of Sport Science. DOI10.1080/17461391.2022.2100279. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35819335/). [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1080/17461391.2022.2100279).

Role: studied triceps comparison, with attention to the biarticular long head. Publisher/search abstract accessible; direct PubMed open returned incomplete content. Does not establish that lower external mass always means lower internal tissue force, nor an exact personal effect size.

## R09 — Anatomy textbook chapters

OpenStax. Anatomy and Physiology 2e. [11.5 Pectoral girdle and upper limbs](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-5-muscles-of-the-pectoral-girdle-and-upper-limbs). [11.6 Pelvic girdle and lower limbs](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-6-appendicular-muscles-of-the-pelvic-girdle-and-lower-limbs).

Role: foundational muscle/action and joint-crossing definitions. Both official chapters accessible. An anatomical action does not by itself quantify task-specific activation, force, fatigue, or longitudinal growth.

## R10 — Original standing/seated calf intervention

Kinoshita M, Maeo S, Kobayashi Y, et al.(2023). Triceps surae muscle hypertrophy is greater after standing versus seated calf-raise training. Frontiers in Physiology 14:1272106. DOI10.3389/fphys.2023.1272106. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38156065/). [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10753835/).

Role: muscle-specific comparison: greater gastrocnemius growth in standing, similar soleus changes between conditions. PubMed abstract accessible. Do not replace the muscle-specific finding with “all calf muscles grew more.”
