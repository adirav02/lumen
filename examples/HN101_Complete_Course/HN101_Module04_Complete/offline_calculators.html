Module 4 — Biomechanics Lab

Five offline tools supporting six lesson labs. These tools calculate or organize supplied information; they do not measure muscle force, diagnose symptoms, or predict individual growth. No data is transmitted or saved. Use Print to retain a worksheet.

1. Single-force moment

Enter known quantities.θ is between radius and force vectors. Uses g=9.81 m/s². Magnitude of one external moment only; other forces and dynamics excluded. A machine stack label is not automatically handle force.

2. Three-position profile comparison

Supply a constant force for each hypothetical setup and three perpendicular distances in start, middle, end order. Positions do not establish muscle length.

Three samples are not a complete resistance curve.

3. ROM description

Use the same defined joint-angle convention for both endpoints. Knee examples use 0° at full extension. This tool reports span only.

An angular span does not determine work, effort, or hypertrophy.The 0–180° domain is a worksheet convention, not a universal anatomical range.

4. Task-specific checklist builder

Unknown is not a diagnosis or a positive finding.

5. Exercise candidate filter

Filtering uses authored action categories and required equipment tags. It does not establish identical resistance profiles, suitability for symptoms, or a ranking. No candidates is a valid result.
