## Lab grading guidance

For each lesson lab, score 0–2 for explicit task/setup, 0–2 for reproducible range/observations, 0–2 for correct numerical or directional reasoning, and 0–2 for acknowledging limits. Keep this 8-point practice rubric separate from the 100-point exam.

For the 4.6 capstone, require four exercise records, two substitution comparisons, and one insufficient-information case. Each substitution must name preserved, changed, and unknown features plus a new baseline. Do not reward a confident exact-profile claim unsupported by geometry.
