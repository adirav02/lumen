Section 1: Bayes' rule / נוסחת בייס
Let D and not-D partition the population; P(D)=0.02 and P(not-D)=0.98.
Let + denote a positive test. Sensitivity P(+|D)=0.90.
False-positive rate P(+|not-D)=0.10. It is NOT P(D|+).
Assume a random person from this population and these exact rates.
P(+)=P(+|D)P(D)+P(+|not-D)P(not-D)=0.018+0.098=0.116>0.
P(D|+)=P(+|D)P(D)/P(+)=0.018/0.116=9/58.
The denominator requires the partition and both likelihoods; sensitivity alone is insufficient.
